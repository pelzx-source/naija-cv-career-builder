Career Tips

Make your application stronger.

01 — Keep your CV clear

Use clear headings, readable spacing and information that is relevant to the opportunity.

02 — Show your projects

Personal projects can demonstrate your abilities, especially when you have limited work experience.

03 — Customize your application

Adjust your CV and cover letter to match the role you are applying for.

04 — Keep learning

Build practical skills through projects, courses, communities and real-world practice.

05 — Quantify achievements

When possible, explain what you achieved using clear results, numbers or outcomes.

06 — Keep your contact details professional

Use an email address and contact information that you are comfortable using for applications.
